did you catch the energy?

$ man gesture-capture

A mannequin strikes a pose and a ring counts down — sweep its line of action in 1–3 flowing strokes while it shows, then press done ✓ and rate your own energy.

Score is soft on purpose: 60% how closely your longest stroke hugs the true action line, 40% your stars — gesture is feel, not tracing.

Studio tip: draw the line the pose would fall along — if it toppled over, which way? One line first, whole body.
